Langle Privacy policy

Learn words from the videos and pages you already enjoy

Langle is a free extension for Chrome. Hover a word on YouTube, Netflix or an article to see what it means in that sentence, save the words you want, and come back to where you met them.

  • Just hoverWords and phrases are coloured by how well you know them. Hover one for its meaning; click it for the dictionary.

  • Your words, with where you met themEach saved word keeps its sentence and the page or video it came from, so you can go back to it.

  • Your data stays yoursLangle has no servers and no account. It saves your words on your device and in your browser's sync, and, only if you connect it, in your own Google Drive.
